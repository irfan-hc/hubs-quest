```

            ▼

                                            ▼
```

## 4. How the WAV Is Generated

1. **Duration & Frame Calculation:**
   $$\text{Duration} = \text{composition.totalDuration} + 1.2\text{ s (reverb tail)}$$
   $$\text{TotalFrames} = \lceil \text{Duration} \times 44100 \rceil$$
2. **Voice Synthesis Matching:**
   - **Pad:** Dual oscillators (Sine + Triangle at $1.002\times$ detune), $0.35\text{ s}$ attack ramp, sustain, exponential release.
   - **Bell:** Sine fundamental + Sine harmonic ($2.76\times$), rapid attack ($0.015\text{ s}$), exponential decay.
   - **Pluck:** Triangle wave, dynamic lowpass filter sweep ($4.5\times \to 1.2\times$), fast decay ($0.008\text{ s}$ attack).
   - **Stereo Panning:** Constant-power panning law:
     $$G_L = \cos\left(\frac{\text{pan} + 1}{2} \times \frac{\pi}{2}\right), \quad G_R = \sin\left(\frac{\text{pan} + 1}{2} \times \frac{\pi}{2}\right)$$
   - **Delay & Reverb Bus:** $0.38\text{ s}$ delay buffer with feedback gain ($0.32$) and warm lowpass filtering.
   - **Master Limiter:** Hyperbolic tangent soft saturation preventing digital clipping:
     $$y(t) = \tanh(x(t))$$
3. **Binary 16-Bit PCM WAV Packing:**
   - Standard 44-byte RIFF/WAVE header:
     - `ChunkID`: `"RIFF"`, `ChunkSize`: $36 + \text{dataSize}$
     - `Format`: `"WAVE"`, `Subchunk1ID`: `"fmt "`
     - `AudioFormat`: `1` (Linear PCM), `NumChannels`: `2` (Stereo)
     - `SampleRate`: `44100` Hz, `ByteRate`: `176400` bytes/sec
     - `BlockAlign`: `4` bytes, `BitsPerSample`: `16` bits
     - `Subchunk2ID`: `"data"`, `Subchunk2Size`: $\text{frames} \times 4$
   - Interleaved 16-bit signed little-endian PCM integers:
     $$\text{sample}_{\text{int16}} = \begin{cases} \text{round}(s \times 32768) & s < 0 \\ \text{round}(s \times 32767) & s \ge 0 \end{cases}$$

## 6. Known Limitations

1. **Large Recursion Tree Durations:** At high recursion depth ($D \ge 9$), composition generation produces up to 60+ simultaneous voices; the voice limiter caps polyphony at 8 to preserve acoustic clarity and reasonable file size ($< 5\text{ MB}$).
2. **Format Scope:** Output is standard uncompressed 16-bit stereo PCM WAV ($44.1\text{ kHz}$). Compressed formats (MP3/OGG) were intentionally avoided to prevent third-party WASM encoder bloat.
