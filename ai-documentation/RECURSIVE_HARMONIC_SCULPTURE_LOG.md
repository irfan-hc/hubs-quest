## 1. Rendering Architecture & How Recursive Data Drives the Sculpture

In `src/lib/visualization/canvasRenderer.ts` (`CanvasTreeRenderer`):

- **`buildSculptureGeometry(result: RecursionResult)`** consumes the unmodified Stage 2 `BranchEvent[]` array:
  - **Symmetry Fold:** Derived directly from `result.config.branchAngle` (`Math.round(240 / branchAngle)` clamped to `4..10`), so changing the Branching Angle slider reshapes the radial symmetry of the entire sculpture.
  - **Root (`depth === 0`):** Forms the central harmonic core at `(0, 0)`.
  - **Parent-Child Connections (`depth >= 1`):** Every `BranchEvent` traces from its parent node `(px, py)` (looked up via `branch.parentId`) along a polar orientation combining `branch.angle`, `branch.order`, and `branch.depth`, with radial segment length `branch.length * 0.72` and curved bezier control point `(ctrlX, ctrlY)` driven by `branch.pan`.
  - **Concentric Orbital Rings:** Computed from the mean radial distance `R_d` of actual `BranchEvent` nodes at each depth `1..maxDepthReached`.
  - **Event ID Preservation:** Every `SculptureNode` stores its exact `branch.id` so `setHighlightedBranches(Set<string>)` illuminates the exact corresponding petal, facet, spiral, and terminal node when its musical note sounds.

## 3. Progressive Formation, Camera, Performance & Accessibility

- **Progressive Formation:** `drawFrame(progress, timestamp)` reveals the central core first (`0.0–0.2`), then unfolds orbital rings and recursive depth layers outward in `branch.depth` / `branch.order`, settling cleanly at `progress = 1.0` and cancelling `requestAnimationFrame` when idle.
- **Reduced Motion:** Detects `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` and renders the completed sculpture (`progress = 1.0`) immediately without animation loops.
- **Scroll & Camera:** Normal wheel scrolling navigates the page; `Ctrl/Cmd + Scroll` or explicit `Zoom In`/`Zoom Out` buttons adjust zoom; drag pans; `Reset View` / double-click resets framing.
